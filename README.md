StudyMate

StudyMate is an AI-powered study productivity platform designed to help students stay focused, organized, and consistent during academic preparation.

Problem

Students often struggle with:

- inconsistent study habits
- poor time management
- burnout
- lack of personalized planning
- when to take break 

StudyMate addresses these challenges using AI-powered productivity tools and analytics.

Solution

StudyMate combines productivity systems with intelligent study planning into one platform. The application helps students manage schedules, maintain consistency, and improve learning efficiency.
